# "הסים שלך" — art credits

- **Fighters, skins, bosses, item icons, arenas and level maps** (`chars/`, `items/`, `worlds/`): original illustrations made for this game.
  - Style set by the app owner's own designs: the black-and-red dragon (`chars/dragon.webp`) and the black-and-orange cat (`chars/cat.webp`).
  - The rest were generated with FLUX.1 [schnell] (Black Forest Labs, Apache-2.0) to match that style, then cut out and cleaned up.
- Background removal: rembg (isnet-anime, isnet-general-use, BiRefNet).
- Older UI icons still in use (stars, ranks, potions) are in `assets/game/items` — see `assets/game/CREDITS.md`.

## Live 3D fighters (`assets/sim3d`)

- Made with Tripo3D (image to 3D from the illustrations above, auto-rig) on the app owner's paid Tripo API account.
- One set of fight animations (Tripo biped presets) was bought on the dragon and copied onto every other biped's skeleton.
